# Attribution — Muscatine WRRF anaerobic co-digestion datasets

The files in this folder are redistributed unchanged from Iowa Research Online
under the **Open Data Commons Attribution License (ODC-By) v1.0**
(<https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1.0>).

Schroer, H. W. and Just, C. L. (2024). *Laboratory, operational, and SCADA
datasets for anaerobic co-digestion at the Muscatine, IA Water Resource Recovery
Facility.* University of Iowa. <https://doi.org/10.25820/data.006715>

Associated article: Schroer, H. W. and Just, C. L. (2023). Feature Engineering
and Supervised Machine Learning to Forecast Biogas Production during Municipal
Anaerobic Co-Digestion. *ACS ES&T Engineering* 3(12).
<https://doi.org/10.1021/acsestengg.3c00435>

Only the small files (README, daily lab file, two data dictionaries) are
committed. `SCADA-raw.csv` (88.8 MB) is fetched by `python -m anchor.fetch` and
is ignored by git. Every file is listed with URL, size and SHA-256 in
`anchor/MANIFEST.json`.
